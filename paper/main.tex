\title{Time-Causal Scale Space from Hemigroup Axioms:\\
Characterization of the Kernels}
\author{Daniel Fagerstr\"om}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We characterize the time-causal scale spaces: the families of smoothing operators through
which an uncommitted observer can measure a temporal signal at all scales at once. The
axioms are those of causal, covariant measurement, with the cascade property stated as a
two-parameter hemigroup, $\Phi_{y,z}\Phi_{x,y} = \Phi_{x,z}$, in place of the classical
one-parameter semigroup --- retaining composition of measurements while dropping the tacit
assumption that measurement stages are interchangeable. The admissible kernel families are
exactly the Laplace exponents of the self-decomposable laws, equivalently the marginals of
self-similar additive (Sato) subordinators; the semigroup case is recovered as the
homogeneous sub-class --- the extremal stable densities together with a pure delay ---
whose heavy tails are thereby exposed
as the price of stage-interchangeability rather than of causality and covariance. The
enlarged class contains members with all moments finite, the Gamma family foremost,
implemented exactly by cascades of first-order filter sections that approximate the entire
completely-monotone subclass from inside the class. Non-creation of temporal structure is
settled in both directions: no nondegenerate member admits scale-monotone non-creation ---
variation-diminishing increments force the pure delay --- while the endpoint form, no
scale showing more sign changes than the signal, is attained exactly by the classified
variation-diminishing members, drift plus integer Thorin atoms. Over the memory half-line, under a mild standing
hypothesis, the theory takes the form of a signaling problem, solved with existence and
uniqueness in an explicit class: a covariant Mellin operator inverts the
scale evolution into an evolution in present time. The memory line stores the analytic
family of Riemann--Liouville integrals of the past signal, and the temporal $N$-jet is an
instantaneous linear readout of that state. Locality together with the positive maximum
principle classifies the admissible memory-line media, unfolding the isolated heat case of
the semigroup theory into a one-parameter Bessel family. The characterization and the
signaling form are machine-checked in the Lean~4 proof assistant, resting on two cited
analytic facts.
\end{abstract}
