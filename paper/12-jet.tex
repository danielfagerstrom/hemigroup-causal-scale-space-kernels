\section{The temporal N-jet: the memory embodies its derivatives}\label{sec:jet}

The output of a scale space is not the smoothed field alone but its jet --- the temporal
derivatives $\partial_t^n u(t, x)$ across scales, the causal analogue of the
derivative-of-Gaussian receptive fields. Computed naively, $\partial_t^n u$ requires
differencing the field in time, i.e.\ a \emph{second} memory recording the recent history of
the scale space on top of the memory line itself --- a history whose length is the window
$T_p$ of Tables~\ref{tab:windows-mean} and~\ref{tab:windows-mode}, since the stencil must
see the kernel's mass. This section shows that the axioms exclude
the need for it: the inversion structure of \S\ref{sec:signaling} turns temporal
differentiation into an instantaneous spatial readout of the present memory state --- the
memory line embodies its own jet.

The hypothesis space iterates the core of \S\ref{sec:preliminaries}: what the theorem needs
of $f$ is that each derivative up to order $n$ is again a causal $L^1$ function that is the
primitive of the next --- the vanishing boundary values are exactly what will turn
$I^{z}f^{(n)}$ into $I^{z-n}f$ in the proof. So let
\[
  \core^n \;:=\; \bigl\{\, f \in \Xz : \text{there are } f^{(1)}, \dots, f^{(n)} \in \Xz
  \text{ with } f^{(j)} = \textstyle\int_0^{\,\cdot} f^{(j+1)},\ 0 \le j < n \,\bigr\},
\]
so that $\core^1 = \core$.

% shared with blueprint def:jet — promoted 2026-09-01 with chapters 13–14.
\begin{definition}\label{def:jet}
The \emph{temporal $N$-jet} of the scale space at $(t, x)$ is
$\{\partial_t^n u(t, x)\}_{0 \le n \le N}$, and the \emph{scale-normalized jet} is
$\{x^n \partial_t^n u(t, x)\}_{n \le N}$, which in the canonical gauge transforms under the
joint dilation $(t, x) \mapsto (\sigma t, \sigma x)$ like the field itself.
\end{definition}

% shared with blueprint thm:embodied-jet — promoted 2026-09-01; "as in §9" became
% "as at order one" in the statement so the two copies share verbatim.
\begin{theorem}[the embodied jet]\label{thm:embodied-jet}
Assume (H), canonical gauge, $f \in \core^n$, and let $u(\cdot, x) = \Phi_{0,x}f$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item For every $t > 0$ and $1 < \operatorname{Re} z < z_*$,
\[
  \widetilde{\partial_t^n u(t, \cdot)}(z) \;=\; \tilde H(z)\,\bigl(I^{z} f^{(n)}\bigr)(t)
  \;=\; \tilde H(z)\,\bigl(I^{\,z - n} f\bigr)(t),
\]
the second equality holding as a Riemann--Liouville integral for $\operatorname{Re} z > n$ and
by analytic continuation --- a fractional derivative of $f$ --- below.
\item Consequently, whenever $z_* > n + 1$, the $n$-th temporal derivative is the $n$-th power
of the inversion operator applied to the \emph{present} memory state: in the Mellin sense on
$n + 1 < \operatorname{Re} z < z_*$,
\[
  \partial_t^n u(t, \cdot) \;=\; A^n\, u(t, \cdot), \qquad
  A^n = \frac{1}{x^n}\,B^{(n)}(\theta), \qquad
  B^{(n)}(-z) := \frac{\tilde H(z + n)}{\tilde H(z)} = \prod_{j=0}^{n-1} B(-z - j)
\]
for $0 < \operatorname{Re} z < z_* - n$ --- the identity evaluating the symbol at the shifted
argument, as at order one.
\end{enumerate}
In particular the temporal jet at time $t$, up to every order $n < z_* - 1$, is an
instantaneous linear functional of the single profile $x \mapsto u(t, x)$; no record of past
states is required. The identity is Mellin-level, with the pointwise reading subject to the
same pole discipline as order one (Remark~\ref{rem:poles}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
(1) By induction on the order: for $j < n$, $f^{(j)}$ is the primitive of
$f^{(j+1)} \in \Xz$, so Lemma~\ref{lem:memory-fractional-integrals}'s derivative clause
applies at each step --- $\partial_t\,\Ex[f^{(j)}(\cdot - xT_1)] = \Ex[f^{(j+1)}(\cdot - xT_1)]$
as $\Xz$-derivatives, the boundary conditions of $\core^n$ licensing each step --- and $n$
steps give $\partial_t^n u(t, x) = \Ex\bigl[f^{(n)}(t - x T_1)\bigr]$. The lemma applied to
the causal $f^{(n)} \in \Xz$ then gives the
first equality; the second is $I^{z} f^{(n)} = I^{\,z-n} I^{\,n} f^{(n)} = I^{\,z-n} f$,
$I^{\,n}f^{(n)} = f$ being the primitive structure of $\core^n$ again. (2) Telescoping the shift relation of
Definition~\ref{def:inversion-operator} $n$ times,
$\Mel[A^n g](z) = \prod_{j=1}^{n} B(j - z)\,\tilde g(z - n)
= \tfrac{\tilde H(z)}{\tilde H(z - n)}\,\tilde g(z - n)$; insert
$\tilde g = \widetilde{u(t,\cdot)}$ from Lemma~\ref{lem:memory-fractional-integrals} and
compare with (1).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[differentiation is re-indexing]\label{rem:reindexing}
Combined with Remark~\ref{rem:embodiment}, Theorem~\ref{thm:embodied-jet}(1) says that the
memory line stores, once and simultaneously, the analytic family $\{(I^{z} f)(t)\}$ of
fractional integrals of the past --- and the jet of every order is a \emph{re-reading} of that
same stored family at shifted index, order $n$ living at Mellin depth
$\operatorname{Re} z > n$ of the strip. Nothing new is stored when one differentiates; the
embodiment of the signal already contains its jet, at every order below $z_* - 1$.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[the Gamma family: jets by subtraction along the shape column]\label{prop:gamma-jets}
For the Gamma family write $u_m(\cdot, x)$ for the field of shape $m$ ($u_0 := f$). From
$s(1 + xs)^{-m} = x^{-1}\bigl[(1 + xs)^{-(m-1)} - (1 + xs)^{-m}\bigr]$,
\[
  x\,\partial_t\, u_m \;=\; u_{m-1} - u_m \;=\; -\,(\nabla u)_m,
  \qquad (\nabla u)_m := u_m - u_{m-1},
\]
and hence, iterating at fixed $x$,
\[
  x^n\,\partial_t^n\, u_\gamma \;=\; (-\nabla)^n u_\gamma
  \;=\; \sum_{j=0}^{n} (-1)^{n-j}\binom{n}{j}\, u_{\gamma - j}(t, x),
  \qquad 0 \le n \le \gamma .
\]
The column $\{u_m(\cdot, x)\}_{m=0}^{\gamma}$ is realized by $\gamma$ identical leaky
integrators in series with time constant $x$, $u_m = (1 + x\,\partial_t)^{-1} u_{m-1}$ --- the
gamma memory of \sscite{devries1992gamma} --- so the scale-normalized jet at scale $x$ is a
binomial-weighted \emph{subtraction of coexisting filter states}, with no temporal
differencing anywhere in the architecture, $\gamma$ states per scale, and jets available up
to order $\gamma$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The transform identity is $(1+xs)^{-m}\bigl[(1+xs) - 1\bigr] = xs(1+xs)^{-m}$; induction in
$n$ at fixed $x$; the realization is Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-cascade} at $x_k = 0$,
where the section degenerates to the pure pole $(1 + x_{k+1}s)^{-1}$.
\end{proof}

The column is the pure-pole cascade from scale zero, fed by the signal
(Figure~\ref{fig:jet-taps}) --- an architecture parallel to the knot ladder of
\S\ref{sec:implementation}, not a reading of its states: the ladder's taps carry only the
top shape $u_\gamma$ at each knot, so jets at $M$ scales cost one column of $\gamma$ states
per scale, the same total count as the ladder, fed from $f$ rather than from the previous
knot. And the identity is exact for all $n \le \gamma$, extending beyond the strip
condition of Theorem~\ref{thm:embodied-jet} (the transforms are rational and continue
meromorphically) --- consistently with $z_* = \gamma$ marking the strip reading and with
the relaxation of hypothesis (H) by meromorphic continuation left open in
\S\ref{sec:conclusions}.

The subtraction rule is the single-atom case. For a Thorin bank
(Proposition~\ref{prop:thorin-subclass}) the field is a convolution of per-node Gamma
fields and no per-column subtraction reads its jet; there the jet is read through
Theorem~\ref{thm:embodied-jet}(2), whose symbol $B^{(n)}$ is again rational for rational
$B$. We do not develop that readout here.

\begin{remark}[receptive fields; Laguerre structure]\label{rem:laguerre}
By Rodrigues' formula the jet kernels are associated Laguerre: with $\phi^{(m)}_x$ the
Gamma density of shape $m$ at scale $x$ and $\phi_x = \phi^{(\gamma)}_x$,
\[
  x^n\,\partial_t^n\, \phi_x \;=\; \frac{n!\,\Gamma(\gamma - n)}{\Gamma(\gamma)}\,
  \phi^{(\gamma - n)}_x\; L_n^{(\gamma - n - 1)}\!\bigl(t/x\bigr),
  \qquad 0 \le n < \gamma,
\]
a Gamma density of shape $\gamma - n$ times an associated Laguerre polynomial --- the
shape dropping as the order rises, each derivative spending one of the kernel's poles.
And the shape column spans the order-$\gamma$ rational function space with the single
pole at $-1/x$: the classical Laguerre filter space at time constant $x$, exponentials
times polynomials of degree $< \gamma$, whose orthogonal basis is the Laguerre
functions. Both clauses are computations; the orthogonality belongs to the basis of the
space, not to the jet kernels themselves, whose polynomial parts all have degree
$\gamma - 1$. The comparison the clauses support is structural: the temporal
receptive-field family stands to the causal scale space
as the Hermite/derivative-of-Gaussian family stands to the Gaussian one --- a graded basis,
read off the embodied state by subtraction. The difference is that the grading here is
by the shape index rather than by differentiation order alone.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[the local corner: derivatives as curvatures of the memory trace]\label{rem:local-jet}
For the Bessel family, $z_* = \infty$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:moment-recursion}(2)), so every order
is available; and in the parabolic gauge $\xi$ of Remark~\ref{rem:gauge-freedom},
Theorem~\ref{thm:embodied-jet}(2) is the $n$-th power of the local operator of
Example~\ref{ex:bessel-quadratic}:
\[
  \partial_t^n u \;=\; \Bigl(\tfrac12\,\partial_\xi^2 + \frac{\beta}{\xi}\,\partial_\xi\Bigr)^{\!n} u,
\]
the jet read from spatial stencils of order $2n$ on the instantaneous memory profile. In the
heat case $\beta = 0$ this is the statement that the observer reads temporal derivatives as
curvatures, and iterated curvatures, of its memory trace --- the embodiment reading already
implicit in \sscite{fagerstrom2005temporal}, Thm.~4, here extended from the local corner to
the whole class by the Mellin form.
\end{remark}

\begin{example}[the machine run end to end]\label{ex:numerical-experiment}
Figures~\ref{fig:experiment} and~\ref{fig:fingerprint} run the whole architecture of
\S\ref{sec:implementation} and this section on one signal: the recursive cascade of
Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-cascade} at $\gamma = 2$, over a geometric knot ladder of $64$
knots spanning $x \in [2, 400]$ (the time unit is one sample; $4096$ samples), with the
jets read by binomial subtraction along the shape column
(Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-jets}) --- no temporal differencing anywhere. The signal
assembles one specimen per prediction: a sharp and a broad bump, a strong event with a
small companion in its wake, a persistent step, and a broadband noise burst. The maps
display the qualitative content of the preceding sections at a glance: every ridge shears
rightward along its mean-delay curve $t_0 + \gamma x$ --- the delay--smoothing coupling of
Theorem~\ref{thm:signaling-form} made visible --- the events annihilate in order of their
widths while the step persists, and the fingerprints close from below without re-opening.

Four checks tie the run to numbered results; both figures and every quoted number are
produced by the accompanying script \texttt{make-fig-experiment.py}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Knot exactness} (Proposition~\ref{prop:knot-exactness}). Refining the ladder
from $64$ to $128$ knots moves the final tap by $4.3 \times 10^{-5}$, and the ladder
agrees with the filter computing the full map $\Phi_{0, x_{\max}}$ in a single increment
to $2.2 \times 10^{-3}$ --- both pure time discretization, the continuum transfers being
identical. A forward-Euler march of the scale evolution, run for contrast, errs by
$1.3 \times 10^{-2}$, halving to $6.0 \times 10^{-3}$ at doubled scale resolution: the
$O(\Delta x)$ that the exponential-integrator update of
Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-cascade} avoids.
\item \emph{Non-creation from the signal} (Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-regularity}). The
extremum count satisfies $N\bigl(u(\cdot, x)\bigr) \le N(f)$ at every one of the $64$
scales ($146$ at the input, at most $94$ across the ladder). The counting convention,
identical for signal and field and implemented in \texttt{count\_extrema}: signs of
successive differences, with a deadband --- differences of magnitude below $10^{-7}$ set
to zero --- and the zero signs then dropped, which compresses flat runs so that a plateau
counts as one extremum, not two; the count is the number of sign alternations that
remain, taken over the sampled window with no padding, so only interior extrema are
counted. Scale-to-scale monotonicity
is not merely unasserted but impossible in the class
(Proposition~\ref{prop:vd-impossibility}) --- the increments carry an atom and transform
zeros and are not variation-diminishing (Remark~\ref{rem:pf-hemigroup}) --- and the run
shows the count rising at $7$ of the $64$ steps. Inspected individually, the created
pairs are micro-events, of prominence $6 \times 10^{-3}$ and below, in the remnant of
the noise burst and near the dissolving wake, where the sampled cascade retains more
high frequency than the continuum kernels (the sections' feedthrough); recomputing the
taps with transform-domain kernels moves the residual increases to the
kernel-discretization floor. The run thus certifies the bound at every scale and shows
step-monotonicity failing in the implemented system, without exhibiting a certified
continuum creation event.
\item \emph{Non-enhancement along the delay path}
(Corollary~\ref{cor:past-dominating}). The tracked maxima of the strong and the coarse
event never increase at any scale where the past-dominating condition holds ($0$
violations; the condition holds at $15$ and $63$ of the $64$ scales respectively). The
wake companion realizes the corollary's sharp failure mode: its maximum decays to
$0.0423$ by $x = 35$, is then lifted $8\%$ to $0.0456$ by the strong event's arriving
delayed mass --- the echo --- and annihilates near $x = 49$ (the inset of
Figure~\ref{fig:experiment}).
\item \emph{Jets by subtraction} (Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-jets}). The identity
$x\,\partial_t u_2 = u_1 - u_2$ holds to $4.8 \times 10^{-17}$ in transform semantics ---
the machine floor --- and the recursive shape column satisfies it against temporal
differencing to $6.7 \times 10^{-3}$, once more the time discretization.
\end{enumerate}

One feature of the maps should be read off the symbol rather than dismissed as an
artifact. In the fully normalized second-order map $x^2 \partial_t^2 u$ the noise burst
leaves fine streaks at \emph{every} scale: the jet operator's transfer
$x^n s^n (1 + xs)^{-\gamma}$ --- here $n = \gamma = 2$ --- behaves as
$(xs)^{n - \gamma}$ at high frequency, so the \emph{margin} $\gamma - n$ is the
roll-off order of the composite filter. At $n = \gamma$ the margin is zero: the
derivative order exhausts the kernel's pole order, the composite is all-pass at high
frequency, and the normalization cancels the scale dependence exactly --- which is why
the streaks keep constant amplitude at every scale instead of fading upward. The top of
the jet is noise-limited by construction; a clean $n$-th
derivative wants poles to spare, $\gamma > n$, consistently with
Proposition~\ref{prop:gamma-jets}'s count of jets available up to order $\gamma$. This
is the Fourier face of the Mellin budget $z_* = \gamma$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:embodied-jet}, both paid for by the kernel's onset behavior
$t^{\gamma - 1}$: onset smoothness, high-frequency decay, and negative-moment
finiteness are one finiteness, and the single number $\gamma$ rations all three.
\end{example}
