\section*{Author contribution and use of AI}

The theory presented here was conceived and originally developed by the author in a series
of publications between 2004 and 2011. This article re-presents, extends, and formally
verifies that work. Generative AI was used as an assistant: for drafting and restructuring
exposition from the author's own earlier manuscripts, for assisting the Lean~4
formalization, and for triaging related literature. The author directed all of this work,
takes full responsibility for the mathematical content and its correctness, and has checked
every citation.

The formal results are machine-checked by the Lean~4 proof assistant and its Mathlib
library; their validity rests on the Lean kernel and is independent of how the proof terms
were produced. The analytic facts taken as axioms in the formalization rather than proved
are enumerated, each with a page-verified citation, in the axiom ledger of the accompanying
repository (\S\ref{sec:machine-checked}); these are the sole human-trust boundary of the
verified development.
