# External reviews

Two referee-style reviews of the monograph, solicited by the author from frontier AI
models in late August 2026 against a then-current PDF build, and archived here verbatim
as provenance. The filenames carry the attribution the author recorded when saving them:
the model and, for the first, its reasoning-effort setting.

- `hemigroup_detailed_review_sol5_6_extra_high.md` — the detailed journal-style review
  ("major revision"). This is *the review* that `../PLAN-review-response.md` responds to,
  finding by finding; its verdict and the disposition of each item are recorded there,
  and the revisions it drove landed through the review-response PRs of 2026-09-01.
- `hemigroup-review-gemini3.6thinking.md` — a shorter dual-perspective review
  (narrative and presentation advice). Two of its presentation items were folded into
  the response plan's R8/R9; the rest is recorded here unactioned.

Page references inside the reviews ("PDF page 33") are to the reviewed build, which
predates the review-response revisions; section and proposition numbers may since have
shifted. The reviews are inputs to the revision process, not statements by the author;
where a review's claim was found wrong or out of scope, the response plan says so.
